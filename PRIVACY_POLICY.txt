PRIVACY POLICY
SaveVault
Last updated: June 2025

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. OVERVIEW

SaveVault is a free, open-source desktop application for Windows that backs up
your video game save files to your own Google Drive account.  It is a personal
utility — it has no backend servers, no user accounts, no analytics, and no
data collection of any kind.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

SaveVault does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.
Specifically:

  - No telemetry or usage statistics are recorded.
  - No crash reports are sent anywhere.
  - No analytics libraries are included.
  - No advertising identifiers are used.
  - No email addresses, names, or any other personally identifiable information
    are collected by the application or its developer.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. GOOGLE ACCOUNT ACCESS

To back up your saves, SaveVault asks you to sign in with your Google account
using the standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen.  SaveVault requests only the
following permission scope:

  https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope limits the application so that it can ONLY see and modify files that
SaveVault itself created.  It cannot read, access, browse, list, modify, or
delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

Your OAuth token (the credential that proves you are signed in) is stored
locally on your own computer at:

  %USERPROFILE%\.savevault\token.json

This file never leaves your machine except through the standard Google OAuth
token-refresh mechanism that communicates directly with Google's servers.  The
developer of SaveVault never receives, sees, or has access to your token,
your email address, or any content of your Google Drive.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. YOUR SAVE FILES

Your save files are uploaded directly from your PC to your own Google Drive
account using the Google Drive API.  They are not routed through any
third-party server.  The developer of SaveVault has no access to your files.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

SaveVault interacts only with Google's OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive v3 APIs.
Your use of Google services is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy:

  https://policies.google.com/privacy

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be committed to the
SaveVault GitHub repository with an updated "Last updated" date above.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7. CONTACT
